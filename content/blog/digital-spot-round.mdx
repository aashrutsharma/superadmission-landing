## The Challenge of Spot Rounds

Spot rounds in Indian college admissions are traditionally chaotic and manual. Students travel across states with physical documents and bank drafts, waiting in packed auditoriums hoping a vacancy opens up in their preferred branch.

Institutions struggle equally: seat matrices change minute by minute as students accept offers elsewhere, leading to vacant seats or over-subscription.

## Architectural Blueprint for Digital Spot Rounds

To transition spot rounds into a transparent, digital-first process, three key components are required:

### 1. Real-Time Seat Matrix Sync

Using distributed event streaming, seat vacancy events must be published in real time. Whenever a candidate forfeits a seat or moves to an upgraded tier, the vacant seat is immediately synchronized across counselling portals.

### 2. Verified Credential Checking

By integrating with **APAAR** and **DigiLocker**, candidate qualifications, caste certificates, and domicile proofs are verified before the round starts, removing in-person verification queues.

### 3. Transparent Algorithmic Allocation

The allocation engine processes preference lists in deterministic batches with public verifiable cryptographic logs, ensuring no backdoor allocations take place.
