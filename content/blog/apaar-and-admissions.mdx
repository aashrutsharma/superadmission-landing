## Introduction to APAAR

The **Automated Permanent Academic Account Registry (APAAR)**, introduced as part of the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, establishes an "Edu-Aadhaar" for every Indian student from pre-primary through higher education.

For admissions infrastructure, APAAR fundamentally solves the verification bottleneck.

## Solving the Verification Bottleneck

Today, students upload scanned PDF marksheets and certificates to dozens of disparate portals. Each university runs redundant physical or manual scrutiny desks.

### 1. Zero-Knowledge Credential Proofs

With APAAR linked to the Academic Bank of Credits (ABC), colleges can programmatically verify whether a student meets the 75% cutoff without demanding raw physical transcripts.

### 2. Elimination of Fake Credentials

Digital signatures backed by issuing boards eliminate fraudulent certificates and forged reservation category claims.

### 3. Frictionless Student Mobility

Credit transfers between institutions become instantaneous, paving the way for multi-entry and multi-exit academic journeys.
