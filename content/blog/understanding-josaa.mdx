## What is JoSAA?

The Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) coordinates seat allocation for admissions to over 100 institutes, including IITs, NITs, IIITs, and other Government Funded Technical Institutes (GFTIs).

Handling over 50,000 engineering seats across hundreds of thousands of applicants, it is one of the most intricate matching problems globally.

## The Matching Algorithm

JoSAA employs a modified version of the Gale-Shapley Stable Marriage Algorithm:

### 1. Choice Filling and Locking

Candidates submit a prioritized list of institute-academic program combinations. Choices must be locked before the algorithm executes.

### 2. Multi-Category Ranks

Candidates are considered in their Common Rank List (CRL) as well as category lists (OBC-NCL, SC, ST, EWS, PwD). Female Supernumerary Quota (FSQ) seats are computed dynamically.

### 3. Floating, Freezing, and Sliding

Between rounds, students can accept an offer while remaining eligible for higher-preference choices in subsequent rounds.

## Opportunities for Next-Generation Infrastructure

Modern admission infrastructure can reduce latency, provide simulated allocation forecasts, and streamline post-allocation document scrutiny with automated verification protocols.
